Know what you are measuring — and what you are not.

01 / SCOPE

A synthetic research demonstrator.

This demo illustrates independent real-world stress testing for Physical AI. It uses illustrated scenes and hand-designed model profiles. No model is connected; no real AI inference occurs.

Person detection is available. Casualty cue detection and tracking are planned. TCCC scenes use synthetic training figures: observable signals only, no diagnosis, triage or treatment advice.

02 / SCORING

Same inputs. Same result.

Domain, model profile, sensor, lighting, altitude, speed, obscuration, observation window and seed determine every detection. The scene, metrics, history and exports share one result.

Recall = TP / (TP + FN). Precision = TP / (TP + FP); defined as 0 when there are no detections. PASS requires the configured recall and false-positive thresholds. Partial detections below the recall threshold are DEGRADED. No detections, too many false positives, or recall below half the selected threshold are FAIL.

03 / TIME

Keep the clocks separate.

Observation time is your selected window. Time to first detection is the first stable synthetic detection. Illustrative transit is 120 m at 12 m/s, separate from the observation fly-by speed.

Time to actionable information includes transit, sufficient detections and processing. It is only achieved when the acceptance thresholds pass. Playback is accelerated; it is not a live mission clock.

04 / LIMITS

A test concept, not validation.

Small synthetic samples cannot establish statistical reliability, certification or real model performance. Thermal is an illustration and cannot see through solid objects.

Blind benchmark — planned / not active in this demo. All scenario data and scoring are available in the browser. Real independent evaluation requires held-out field data and server-side scoring.
