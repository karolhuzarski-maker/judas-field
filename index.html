01 / SCOPE

A synthetic research demonstrator.

This demo illustrates independent real-world stress testing for Physical AI. It uses AI-generated training photographs, manually reviewed person annotations and hand-designed model profiles. The synthetic bench runs no real AI inference. Evaluate your model scores locally imported predictions from an external detector against the fixed reference images.

Person detection is available. Casualty cue detection and tracking are planned. TCCC scenes use synthetic training figures: observable signals only, no diagnosis, triage or treatment advice.

02 / SCORING

Same inputs. Same result.

Domain, model profile, sensor, lighting, altitude, speed, obscuration, observation window and seed determine every detection. The scene, metrics, history and exports share one result.

Recall = TP / (TP + FN). Precision = TP / (TP + FP); defined as 0 when there are no detections. PASS requires the configured recall and false-positive thresholds. Partial detections below the recall threshold are DEGRADED. No detections, too many false positives, or recall below half the selected threshold are FAIL.

03 / TIME

Keep the clocks separate.

Effective observation time is calculated from altitude, ground speed and an assumed 60° along-track FOV, limited by a separate cap. Hover has continuous geometric visibility and uses the cap. Time to first detection is the first stable synthetic detection. Illustrative transit is 120 m at 12 m/s, separate from the observation fly-by speed.

Time to actionable information includes transit, sufficient detections and processing. It is only achieved when the acceptance thresholds pass. Playback pace follows fly-by speed; it is a time-compressed preview. The UAV geometry panel separates ground footprint, geometric visibility and effective acquisition time. The FOV is a demonstrator assumption, not a verified DJI specification.

04 / LIMITS

A test concept, not validation.

Small synthetic samples cannot establish statistical reliability, certification or real-world model performance. Synthetic thermal representations are not calibrated radiometric recordings and cannot see through solid objects.

Blind benchmark — planned / not active in this demo. All scenario data and scoring are available in the browser. Real independent evaluation requires held-out field data and server-side scoring.

OPEN BENCHMARK / AVAILABLE EXAMPLES

Know the reference. Test your detector.

Download an annotated synthetic scene, run your model separately and import its predictions. Inspect one-to-one IoU matches, missed people and false alarms. Reference images and annotations are public.

BLIND BENCHMARK / PLANNED

Test beyond familiar data.

The future service will use independently maintained, held-out scenarios to examine generalization beyond familiar development data. This public browser demo does not provide blind or independently verified validation.

Initial specialization: MCI, TCCC and SAR. Registered real RGB/thermal field recordings are a future validation step.
